Call flowUse with the poster

Live-call decision path

The poster starts at the formal introduction. Add Step 0 before that when a gatekeeper answers.

0

Confirm restaurant and decision maker

Say: Hi, is this [Restaurant Name]? Great, am I speaking with [Decision Maker Name]? Do not mention DoorDash until the right person confirms.

1

Mandatory intro after confirmation

Say: This is [Your Name], your DoorDash Account Manager, with DoorDash from a Recorded Line. Do you have two minutes?

2

Set the role in one sentence

Explain that you support the active DoorDash partnership across storefront, operations, support needs, and growth opportunities.

3

Discovery before recommendation

Ask one or two questions about order volume, goals, blockers, slow hours, repeat customers, or missing photos.

4

Recommend one next action

Tie the recommendation to what the merchant said. Keep it specific: photos, video, Sponsored Listings, Promos, Loyalty, or support case follow-up.

5

Close and log

Recap the decision, confirm owner and timing, send the recap, then select the correct Outreach Purpose and Disposition.
