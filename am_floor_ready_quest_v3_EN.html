AM Floor-Ready Quest

From onboarding to floor-ready.

An interactive practice lab for new Account Managers to build tool fluency, merchant diagnosis, consultative discovery, campaign judgment, objection handling and QA discipline before taking live volume.

Purpose: this does not replace the official Playbook. It turns training content into observable practice. Each mission asks the AM to decide what to review, what to ask, what to recommend, what to escalate and how to close a clear next step with a fictional merchant.

How to use it

Practice first. Theory becomes useful when it changes behavior.

The AM advances through missions. Each mission creates evidence: decisions, weak spots, roleplay notes and a coaching summary to share with the TL. Progress is saved locally in this browser.

For the AM

Do the games without checking the answer first. Then review the feedback and repeat the modules where you struggle. The goal is not to “pass”; the goal is to know what to do when the merchant does not follow the perfect script.

For the TL / Coach

The output should be read individually. Two AMs can have the same score and need very different coaching: one may need tool navigation support; another may talk too much and ask too little discovery.

Coaching principle: use this as a formative diagnosis, not as a punishment tool. The score opens a conversation; it does not replace QA, shadowing or live-call feedback.

0-100 roadmap

The AM readiness curve.

The sequence moves from “I know where to click” to “I can diagnose a merchant, run a consultative conversation and defend a recommendation with evidence.”

0-20

Toolbelt

Recognize when to use Merchant Portal, Salesforce, DDMx, Dime, Mint/Figment, Outreach, Slack and approved internal resources.

20-40

Merchant diagnosis

Read Product Mix, Operation Quality, Customer Insights, photos, ratings, sales trends and campaign reporting.

40-60

Consultative call

Open with value, ask permission, run discovery, use SPIN and confirm concrete next steps.

60-80

Growth levers

Choose between photos, descriptions, Sponsored Listings, promotions, Smart Campaigns, Loyalty and dayparting.

80-100

Floor simulation

Solve scenarios involving objections, support friction, campaigns and closing discipline without breaking QA or trust.

A floor-ready AM does not recite features; they connect a business signal to a concrete action and a clear next step.

Game 1

Flip flashcards.

Core concepts with the original term and a practical field translation. Tap each card to see how to use it on a call.

Game 2

Memory: right tool, right use.

The goal is to build an internal resource map. Not every issue belongs in the same system or requires the same case path.

Matches: 0/8 Attempts: 0 Completion bonus
Game 3

Kahoot-style Sprint Quiz.

Fast questions on training week, the Playbook mindset and campaign logic. The correct answer matters; the feedback matters more.

Question 1/1 Score: 0
Game 4

Rejection Dojo: objection vs. recommended action.

The AM practices the difference between reacting, pushing and consulting. A strong response validates the concern, returns to the evidence and proposes a small next step.

Game 5

Flip score cards.

Every restaurant is fictional. Diagnose first. Then flip the card and compare your approach against a floor-ready recommendation.

Game 6

Campaign Lab: choose the right lever.

Not every campaign solves the same problem. This lab forces the AM to separate acquisition, retention, AOV, visibility, conversion and operational capacity.

Recommendation
Select an objective and context.

The output will give you a recommendation, the rationale, a risk to validate and a discovery question.

Game 7

Call Flow Builder.

Put the consultative call steps in order. This trains structure: do not sell before discovery, do not close without a next step, and do not miss QA basics.

Use the arrows to move steps. On mobile this is more reliable than drag-and-drop.
Game 8

Mini roleplay: choose the AM response.

This module simulates call moments. The right response is not the prettiest one; it is the one that combines confidence, discovery, evidence and a next action.
